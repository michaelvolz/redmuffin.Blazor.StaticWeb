I want to expand the sidenote feature. I want these notes to serve as a basis for thinking about features, problems, or ideas.

I want to add comments to them — as many as I like — and attach references, including other files with information or data that could help in the process. It should all be very simple to use. The skill must not become bloated.

I also want to extract knowledge from the excellent compound engineering skill. If there is anything valuable in its process, tactics, format, or OpenCode usage that could benefit us, we should investigate it thoroughly.

The sidenote feature is something I have always wanted: a way to organize my thinking and use that as a basis for brainstorming, planning, or working, which we already do well. That is an enormous time-saver, and I no longer lose my ideas. I could have done so much more if I had written them down the moment I had them.

Now I can, at least during coding. That is why it is so important that the skill adds a new note as frictionlessly and quickly as possible, then immediately continues what we were doing and forgets all about what I typed into the sidenote. It is not important after the sidenote is created. We have it saved for the future.
