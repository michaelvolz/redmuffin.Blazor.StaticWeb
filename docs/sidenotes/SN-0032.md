title: "Research ways to make ConfigureAwait less painful for LLMs in OpenCode"
status: pending
---

ConfigureAwait(false) is verbose and costs significant tokens for LLMs.
Options to investigate: .editorconfig to suppress CA2007 in projects where
it is unnecessary (Blazor WASM, console apps), Roslyn auto-fix that adds it
automatically, global using for a Task-like wrapper, or a project-level
convention that reduces the boilerplate. Goal: zero-token overhead while
maintaining correctness.
